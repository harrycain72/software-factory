# Lunch Roulette

Colleagues in one organisation opt in to have lunch with a few randomly chosen
colleagues at their office, in recurring weekly rounds.

## Language

**Colleague**:
A person in the organisation who can take part in lunches.
_Avoid_: User, employee, member, participant

**Location**:
An office where Colleagues have lunch together; every Colleague has one home Location, and only Colleagues at the same Location are matched.
_Avoid_: Office, site, city

**Round**:
One weekly cycle at a Location: Sign-ups open, the Match runs, and the Lunch Groups have lunch.
_Avoid_: Cycle, batch, week, event

**Sign-up**:
A Colleague's opt-in to a single Round; it does not carry over to the next Round.
_Avoid_: Registration, enrolment, RSVP, subscription

**Match**:
The random draw at the end of a Round's sign-up period that splits the signed-up Colleagues into Lunch Groups, keeping apart Colleagues who recently shared a Lunch Group.
_Avoid_: Pairing, shuffle, draw, assignment

**Lunch Group**:
The Colleagues the Match puts together to have lunch in a Round: 3 or 4, or 5 when exactly five signed up.
_Avoid_: Pair, team, table, party

**Host**:
The member of a Lunch Group, chosen at random by the Match, who is asked to suggest where to eat and start the conversation; a nudge, not a permission.
_Avoid_: Organiser, leader, captain

**Lunch Slot**:
The fixed day and time each Round's Lunch Groups at a Location have lunch; the Lunch Group chooses where.
_Avoid_: Meeting time, appointment, booking

**Cancelled Round**:
A Round in which fewer than three Colleagues signed up at its Location, so no Lunch Groups are formed.
_Avoid_: Empty round, failed round

**Withdrawal**:
A Colleague leaving their Lunch Group after the Match and before its Lunch Slot; nobody replaces them, and a Lunch Group left with one Colleague is dissolved.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, drop-out, leave

**Admin**:
A Colleague who manages Locations and their weekly schedules; takes no part in running individual Rounds.
_Avoid_: Organiser, moderator, owner
